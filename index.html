The Menstrual Cycle & Athletic Performance

A free awareness and education resource for female athletes, coaches, trainers, and parents — built to close the knowledge gap around how the menstrual cycle affects training, performance, and recovery.

Start Learning I'm a Coach

Why this matters

Despite a 15% increase in female Olympic participation over the past two decades, women remain underrepresented in sport and exercise medicine research. Much of this comes down to researchers avoiding the complexity of the menstrual cycle — leaving athletes and coaches with little practical guidance on how it affects performance.

In interviews with athletes and coaches, we found most people knew the names of the cycle phases but not when they occur, how long they last, or how they connect to training, injury risk, and recovery. This site is designed to close that gap.

Who this is for

Female Athletes (17–25)

Understand your own cycle and how to apply that knowledge to training and recovery.

Coaches & Trainers

Learn to recognize performance patterns tied to the cycle and support athletes without added pressure or stigma.

Parents & Organizations

Build awareness to support younger athletes and shape better team policies.

What you'll find here

  • The Cycle — the four phases and their symptoms
  • Athletic Impacts — strength, injury risk, and nutrition across the cycle
  • Coping & Support — practical ways to manage symptoms during training
  • For Coaches — a pre-season refresher on supporting athletes
  • Test Your Knowledge — a short self-check
  • Surveys — help us measure whether this resource works
This resource is for educational purposes as part of a university knowledge mobilization capstone project. It is not medical advice. If you have concerns about your cycle or symptoms, speak with a healthcare provider.
