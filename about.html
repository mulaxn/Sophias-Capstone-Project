About This Project

This site is a knowledge mobilization project created for HUBI 4800: Human Biosciences Capstone at Memorial University of Newfoundland, Fall 2026.

Team (Group 19): Abigail Connors, Jocelyn Setiawan, Kaylie Sandbrook, Sophia Eastman

The project addresses the underrepresentation of women in sport and exercise medicine research, and the resulting lack of awareness among athletes and coaches about how the menstrual cycle affects training and performance. Through interviews with a women's rugby team and two coaches, we identified a clear knowledge gap: no one we spoke with knew all of the information we set out to share.

This site is our knowledge translation product — an accessible, text-based resource (no video required) that anyone can use on their own time, paired with pre/post surveys to measure whether it actually improves understanding.
