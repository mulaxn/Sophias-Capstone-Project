The Cycle

How many phases does the menstrual cycle have, and what are they?

Four: the menstrual phase, follicular phase, ovulation, and the luteal phase.

Which phase has the highest estrogen levels?

Estrogen peaks right before ovulation.

Physical

During which phase is injury risk generally reported to be higher?

The luteal phase, according to several studies — see Athletic Impacts.

When might strength gains be easier to achieve?

The late follicular phase or around ovulation, when estrogen is higher.

Mental

Which phase is most commonly linked to mood changes and fatigue?

The luteal phase, due to rising progesterone and shifting estrogen.

When do most people notice improved mood and energy?

During the follicular phase, as estrogen gradually rises.

Coping

Name two strategies that can help manage symptoms during training.

Examples: cycle tracking, adjusting training load, prioritizing nutrition, proactive pain management, and open communication with a coach. See Coping & Support for more.

What's one reason athletes might hesitate to tell a coach about their cycle?

Stigma, discomfort discussing it with a male coach, or fear it will be seen as an excuse rather than useful information (Brown et al., 2020).
