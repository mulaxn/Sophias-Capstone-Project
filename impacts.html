Athletic Impacts Across the Cycle

Research shows the menstrual cycle can influence strength, injury risk, and nutritional needs. Findings vary between studies and individuals, but general patterns have emerged.

Strength

Some studies show an increase in strength in certain muscle groups during the late follicular phase or around ovulation, when estrogen is higher.

Injury Risk

Several studies have found a potential link between injury risk and cycle phase, with many showing increased risk during the luteal phase.

Nutritional Needs

Nutrient metabolism and appetite shift across phases. Meeting your nutrient needs consistently — even as appetite changes — is important for performance and recovery.

Feel Good = Play Good

Based on a study by Armour et al. (2020) of Australian athletes:

82% reported menstrual pain
58% perceived an effect on performance
<25% adjusted their training in response

That gap — between feeling an effect and actually adjusting training — is a big part of why this resource exists.

Sources: Armour et al. (2020); Pournasiri et al. (2023); Soliman et al. (2024); Furu et al. (2026); Parker et al. (2024); Osmani et al. (2026); Barlow et al. (2024); Rogan & Black (2023); Hackney et al. (1994); Willett et al. (2021). Findings differ across studies and individuals — use this as general context, not a personal prediction.
